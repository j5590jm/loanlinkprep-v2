Frequently Asked Underwriting Questions

What is the SBA Credit Elsewhere rule?

The SBA requires lenders to certify that the borrower does not have the ability to obtain the requested funds on reasonable terms from non-federal sources, including excess personal or corporate liquidity.

How do I legally shield working capital reserves during SBA underwriting?

Document committed 12-month capital expenditures, inventory purchases, and contractual reserves in a signed CapEx Encumbrance Schedule.
