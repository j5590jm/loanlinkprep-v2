How long does a lender have to remove a UCC-1 lien in Pennsylvania?

Under Pennsylvania UCC § 9-513(c), the secured party must file or send a UCC-3 termination statement within 20 days of receiving an authenticated demand from the debtor.

Can I file a UCC-3 termination statement myself in Pennsylvania?

Yes. If the secured party fails to respond within 20 days of your authenticated demand, UCC § 9-509(d)(2) permits the debtor to file the termination statement directly with the Pennsylvania Secretary of State.
